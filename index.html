About

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the AI Thrust at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou).

Note

Prospective students are welcome to contact me by email.

Research

My research centers on machine learning for structured scientific data such as sequences, graphs and point clouds. Current directions include:

  • foundation models for structured data;
  • generative models over structured spaces, such as graph generation;
  • theoretical understanding of graph neural networks and transformers;
  • reliable AI for biomedical applications: molecular/protein foundation models, biomolecule design, antimicrobial resistance detection and mass spectrometry analysis.

Background

  • 2026 - Now: Assistant Professor, AI Thrust, HKUST (Guangzhou). AI advisor at CertiSen.
  • 2023 - 2026: Project leader of the group “Machine Learning on Graphs” in the Department of Machine Learning and Systems Biology, Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry.
  • 2021 - 2023: Postdoc in the Machine Learning & Computational Biology Lab of Karsten Borgwardt at ETH Zürich.
  • PhD (2017 - 2020): Inria and Université Grenoble Alpes, in the THOTH team and the LBBE lab, supervised by Julien Mairal and Laurent Jacob. I worked on (deep) kernel methods for structured data such as sequences and graphs, and their applications in bioinformatics. Funded by AMX.
  • Education: Bachelor and Master at École Polytechnique, and a second Master in applied mathematics (MVA) at ENS Paris-Saclay.
